# REMOVES AI LOOK FROM WEBSTITES

## AS AN AI YOU MUST AVOID EACH AND EVERY POINTS LISTED BELOW

### Visual Design & Color Scheme
1. **Purple-to-blue / Indigo-to-violet gradients:** Using diagonal purple, violet, indigo, or cyan gradients in hero backgrounds, buttons, and callout boxes.
2. **Gradient headline text:** Clipping a multi-color gradient directly inside the text of large headlines, which often degrades legibility.
3. **Cards with a colored left border:** Adding a thin 4-pixel accent stripe or gradient down the left edge of card containers.
4. **Frosted-glass / Glassmorphism panels:** Overlaying semi-transparent panels with backdrop blur over gradient backgrounds.
5. **Low-contrast dark mode:** Pairing mid-grey body text with slightly-less-mid-grey dark backgrounds (such as slate-900), failing basic contrast standards.
6. **Single default font everywhere:** Setting an entire site in a single neutral sans-serif (such as Inter, DM Sans, or Roboto) across similar weights without clear typographic hierarchy.
7. **One italic serif word for effect:** Placing a single word in an italic serif font inside an otherwise sans-serif headline to signal craft or elegance.
8. **Overused trendy font pairings:** Relying on identical display-plus-sans font combinations popular in online design roundups.
9. **Single-color register palette:** Setting icons, tiles, cards, and borders in monochromatic shades of the same color register, eliminating visual contrast.
10. **Grain texture overlays:** Adding a global noise layer across the page to make flat gradients appear filmic.
11. **Cursor-following glow effect:** Using a radial light beam that tracks pointer movement across the hero section.
12. **Hyperrealistic AI stock humans:** Utilizing synthetic stock imagery of unnervingly symmetrical humans with flawless skin and artificial eyes.
13. **Abstract filler visuals:** Substituting real product photos or team imagery with abstract 3D spheres, neon loops, or Lottie animations.

### UI Components & Layout Structure

14. **Three icon boxes in a row:** Forcing feature presentations into exactly three equally weighted icon cards to fit a 12-column grid.
15. **Floating badge pill above headlines:** Positioning a small pill badge reading "AI-Powered", "New", "Announcing v2.0 ✨", or "Backed by Y Combinator" directly above hero headings.
16. **Emojis instead of vector icons:** Using emojis (such as 🚀, 💡, or ✨) as functional header icons or feature bullet points instead of proper SVG icons.
17. **Uniform thin outline icons:** Applying thin outline icons at a single stroke weight that only vaguely fit their target concepts.
18. **Pastel tile icons above every heading:** Placing an icon inside a small rounded pastel tile above every single heading across a page.
19. **Cards nested inside cards:** Wrapping single lines of text or components in nested bordered containers instead of establishing actual visual hierarchy.
20. **Identical dashboard metric cards:** Displaying rows of numerical cards of equal visual weight on a dashboard without emphasizing key metrics.
21. **Unchanged stock buttons:** Retaining framework default button radii, default drop shadows, and basic opacity-fade hover states (e.g., opacity: 0.8).
22. **Centered hero section layout:** Defaulting to a centered headline, subhead, button group, and background gradient.
23. **Standard monochromatic logo strip:** Incorporating a horizontal strip of gray logos labeled "Trusted by".
24. **Dark CTA section near the footer:** Placing a dark-themed call-to-action banner near the bottom of a light page.
25. **Predictable template section order:** Following the rigid section sequence of Hero → Logo Strip → 3 Feature Cards → Testimonials → Pricing → Dark CTA → Footer.
26. **Rigid mathematical symmetry:** Using exact 2x2 or 3-column card layouts where every card contains identical sentence and word counts.
27. **Excessive uppercase eyebrow labels:** Placing monospaced, uppercase text labels directly above section titles.
28. **Decorative status dots with no purpose:** Incorporating colored status dots (green, yellow, red) that do not connect to any real metric or status.
29. **Excessive use of chips/badges:** Encapsulating every category, tag, or piece of metadata inside styled chip elements.
30. **Every inch of space filled:** Overcrowded layouts that pack cards, dividers, tags, and instructions into every open space.
31. **Responsive-by-default empty padding:** Applying massive vertical padding (such as `py-24`) that creates vast empty voids when viewed on desktop screens.

### Copywriting, Tone & Typography

32. **Ubiquitous em dashes (—):** Overusing long em dashes across headlines, body copy, and cards to bridge fragmented thoughts.
33. **Tell-tale AI buzzwords:** Overusing words like "delve", "seamless", "robust", "elevate", "empower", "cutting-edge", "tapestry", and "leverage".
34. **Overexplaining UI elements:** Adding paragraphs of supporting text to simple buttons, cards, or navigation links that need no explanation.
35. **Parallel phrasing ("It's not X, it's Y"):** Repeating parallel structures like "It's not about X, it's about Y" or "Build faster — without sacrificing quality".
36. **Blogging clichés and canned intro hooks:** Relying on cliché hooks like "In today's fast-paced world", "It goes without saying", or "Without further ado".
37. **Testimonials with generic placeholder names:** Featuring fake reviews attributed to names like "John Smith", "Sarah Johnson", or "Alex Miller" with vague titles like "Head of Operations".
38. **Typographic smart quotes (“ ”):** Formatted curly quotes in HTML source code pasted directly from AI outputs instead of standard keyboard straight quotes.
39. **Excessive hedging language:** Using non-committal words such as "typically", "more often than not", "might be", or "don't always".
40. **Exclusive 2nd and 3rd person voice:** Writing entirely in 2nd or 3rd person without natural first-person human perspective or anecdotal shifts.
41. **Mononymously titled 'Conclusion' sections:** Ending pages with sections titled simply "Conclusion" containing exaggerated platitudes.
42. **Unnatural personification:** Giving human agency to non-human tools or concepts without restraint.
43. **Breathless clipped sentence rhythm:** Using short, clipped sentences throughout without natural human variations in sentence length.
44. **Overuse of double adjectives & adverbs:** Pairing two adjectives together or overloading copy with adverbs like "truly" and "quietly".
45. **Capitalizing every word in headers:** Automatically capitalizing the initial letter of every single word in subheadings.

### Interactivity, Animations & Accessibility

46. **Indiscriminate scroll fade-in animations:** Applying fade-in animations across every page section, causing delayed or blank screens on slow connections.
47. **Missing off-happy-path UI states:** Lacking custom designs for empty states ("No items"), loading skeletons, validation errors, partial failure panels, or permission denied screens.
48. **Accessibility failures:** Text contrast falling below 4.5:1, missing focus rings, small tap targets under 24x24 pixels, or layouts breaking at 320px widths.
49. **Keyboard navigation traps & ARIA misapplication:** Misapplying ARIA roles or trapping keyboard users in navigation loops.
50. **Left-border radius clipping glitches:** Applying a colored left border to a container with rounded corners, producing a squared-off visual corner artifact.
